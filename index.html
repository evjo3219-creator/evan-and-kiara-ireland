Happy Birthday, Evan

Birthday in Dublin

  • Ease in after the red-eye — afternoon tea at The Shelbourne or The Merrion, cozy and a little fancy.
  • Birthday dinner: The Winding Stair (views over Ha'penny Bridge) or splurge on Michelin-starred Chapter One.
  • Birthday pints at a Victorian classic — The Long Hall, Kehoe's, or Toner's.
  • Nightcap under the Christmas lights with mulled wine to close out the day.
Fri Dec 4 · treat yourself 🎂
🥂
Fri Dec 4 · Pick One & Book It

Birthday Dinner Shortlist

  • Chapter One — two Michelin stars, the big-splurge tasting menu. Book weeks ahead.
  • The Winding Stair — romantic Irish cooking above the Ha'penny Bridge.
  • Delahunt — refined Irish in a gorgeous Victorian room.
  • Etto — tiny, buzzy, incredible small plates (reserve early).
  • FX Buckley — proper Irish steakhouse if you want a birthday steak.
Fri Dec 4 · reserve ASAP 🥂
📚
Fri Dec 4 · Ireland's Oldest University

Trinity College & the Book of Kells

  • See the 1,200-year-old Book of Kells, hand-illuminated by monks.
  • Stand in the breathtaking Long Room library — 200,000 old books.
  • Wander the cobbled campus in the heart of the city.
  • Perfect first-day, jet-lag-friendly outing.
Fri · right after arrival
🍺
Sat Dec 5 · St. James's Gate

Guinness Storehouse

  • Climb seven floors of the iconic pint-glass atrium.
  • Learn to pour the perfect pint at the Guinness Academy.
  • Toast at the Gravity Bar — 360° views over all of Dublin.
  • Book timed tickets online in advance to skip the queue.
Sat afternoon · ~2 hours
🎻
Sat Dec 5 · Dublin After Dark

Temple Bar & Trad Music

  • Cobblestone lanes, red facades, and live trad sessions every night.
  • Pints at The Temple Bar, oysters, and impromptu sing-alongs.
  • Duck into The Auld Dubliner or Gogarty's for the music.
  • Great date-night energy — go later, stay for the craic.
Sat night · with Kiara 💛
🏞️
Sun Dec 6 · The Wild Atlantic Way

Cliffs of Moher Day Trip

  • 702 ft of sheer cliffs plunging into the Atlantic.
  • Easiest as a guided day tour from Dublin (approx. 12 hrs).
  • Many tours add Galway and the moon-like Burren.
  • Book ahead — the single most jaw-dropping day of the trip.
Sun · full day · dress warm
🥃
Mon Dec 7 · Bow St., Smithfield

Jameson Distillery

  • Guided tour through the original 1780 distillery.
  • Guided whiskey tasting — compare Irish, Scotch & bourbon.
  • Cap it with a cocktail at JJ's Bar.
  • A cozy, warm afternoon activity for the winter chill.
Mon · book the Bow St. Experience
🏰
Mon Dec 7 · History & Stone

Dublin Castle & St. Patrick's

  • Explore Dublin Castle, 800 years at the city's core.
  • Marvel at St. Patrick's Cathedral, Ireland's largest.
  • Nearby: Christ Church and the medieval quarter.
  • Optional: Kilmainham Gaol for the deep history buffs.
Mon morning
🎄
Any Evening · December Magic

Christmas Markets & Lights

  • Mulled wine & stalls at the Dublin Christmas markets.
  • Glittering lights down Grafton & Henry Street.
  • Warm up with hot chocolate and roasted chestnuts.
  • The whole city sparkles — the best part of a winter trip.
Every evening · festive & free
🌊
Free Morning · 30 Mins by DART

Howth Cliff Walk

  • Seaside village with dramatic coastal cliff trails.
  • The freshest fish & chips right on the pier.
  • Spot seals in the harbour and a working lighthouse.
  • Easy half-day escape if we want a quieter morning.
Flexible · weather permitting
